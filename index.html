About the Department

The Department of Physical Education under the College of Education of Central Mindanao University provides instruction and learning experiences in physical education, movement, sports, fitness, and related socio-cultural activities.

The department supports the development of future physical education teachers through its Bachelor of Physical Education program. It also contributes to the physical education learning experiences of students from other degree programs.

Through instruction, research, extension, and community engagement, the department aims to promote physical activity, professional competence, and lifelong learning.

Our Academic Program

Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd) prepares future educators with knowledge and skills in physical education, movement instruction, teaching, and related professional practices.
