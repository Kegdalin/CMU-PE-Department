Objectives of the Physical Education Department

  1. To provide training in Physical Education and related socio-cultural activities of CMU students;
  2. To contribute to Physical Education Theory and Practice through research activities;
  3. To develop awareness of the natural environment and the need for its production and conservation through outdoor and aquatic activities; and
  4. To promote Physical Education through an outreach program in sports, physical fitness and socio-cultural activities.

Objectives of the BPEd Program

  1. Glocally competent and passionate teachers engaged in the holistic development of learners;
  2. Effective implementers and facilitators of programs and services at all educational levels imbued with desirable values as well as pedagogical content knowledge and skills essential to the development of learners; and,
  3. Actively engaged in research, development, extension and expert services on various educational program.

Introduction/History of the BPEd Program

The Department of Physical Education (DPE) is one of the oldest academic units of the College of Education of Central Mindanao University (CMU). Its history is closely interwoven with the growth of the University itself — from a vocational agricultural school into a leading ASEAN university — and today it is home to the Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd) program, which is currently undergoing accreditation by the Accrediting Agency of Chartered Colleges and Universities in the Philippines (AACCUP).

Historical Roots

CMU began in 1946 as the Bukidnon National Agricultural School (BNAS), where, influenced by the American educational system, Physical Education and Health were already part of the curriculum. In 1952, BNAS was elevated into the Mindanao Agricultural College (MAC) by virtue of Republic Act No. 807, with Physical Education operating as one of the units of the Department of Agricultural Education. When MAC was converted into Central Mindanao University in 1965 through Republic Act No. 4498, the Department of Agricultural Education became the College of Education, and the Department of Physical Education continued as one of its units headed by a Chairperson. In 1970, the Board of Regents approved the Bachelor of Science in Education (BSED), while the DPE maintained its role as the service unit delivering physical education across all colleges of the University.

A Tradition of Service and Leadership

Consistent with the University's trilogy of functions in instruction, research, and extension, the DPE committed itself to service physical education for the entire University. For decades, its faculty and students carried out their “labor of love” — training and coaching athletes and leading the University's sports, socio-cultural, physical fitness, and recreational life, from Calisthenics in the 1960s, to Siglakas in the 1970s–90s, Hataw in the 1990s–2010s, and Zumba at present. The Department actively represented CMU in regional and national athletic and socio-cultural competitions (SCUAA), promoting the image of the University while serving as the source of its sports and recreation materials, equipment, and expertise.

From a Major to a Degree Program

The Department steadily built toward a full degree program — organizing the Siglakas Club (1972), conducting sports clinics and seminar-workshops, and sending faculty on MSPE scholarships to the University of the Philippines, Diliman (1995, 1996). In 1998, the College of Education offered the Bachelor of Secondary Education, major in Physical Education (BSED–PE). The first batch was recognized by the University Academic Council in 2002 and posted a remarkable 99% passing rate in the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET). Over the following years the program produced its first Cum Laude graduates and its first sports-related coaches, establishing a solid foundation of academic and athletic excellence.

Birth of the Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd) Program

In August 2017, in response to CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) No. 80, series of 2017, Prof. Procopio B. Galendez Jr. spearheaded the proposal to transform the BSED major in Physical Education into a stand-alone Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd). On February 26, 2018, the Board of Regents approved the revised curriculum, and the BPEd was offered effective School Year 2018–2019. The curriculum underwent a minor revision in 2019 and a further revision in 2023 that adopted the PATHFIT 1–4 framework for the tertiary service courses. When the COVID-19 pandemic struck in 2020, the Department sustained the program by delivering it fully online.

Growth, Distinctions, and Leadership

The BPEd program has grown rapidly in both quality and reputation. Its first batch of graduates in 2022 produced 3 Magna Cum Laude and 39 Cum Laude graduates. The Department went on to produce its first-ever LET topnotcher in 2025 (Mr. Kenneth John N. Batausa, Top 7), followed in 2026 by two more topnotchers (Ms. Juvy Cristina M. Rosales, Top 5, and Mr. Hezziel N. Delima, Top 9). The Department has been led by its Chairpersons — Marylou C. Viloria, Crispin S. Quilang (2012), and Procopio B. Galendez Jr. (2015, and again from August 2025), who in 2023 became the first Doctorate degree holder of the Department. Under his leadership, the CMU Varsity Sports Scholarship Program was approved by the Board of Regents on November 26, 2025 and fully implemented in Academic Year 2026–2027.

The BPEd Program Under Accreditation

As a measure of its commitment to quality assurance, the Bachelor of Physical Education program is under accreditation by AACCUP. Building on the program's strong licensure performance, its expanding roster of honor graduates and topnotchers, and its growing research, extension, and sports-development initiatives, the Department is pursuing AACCUP Level IV, Cycle 2 accreditation (4th Survey) — affirming the program's alignment with national standards and with CMU's vision of the total development of people. The Bachelor of Physical Education program continues to grow, honing each prospective physical education teacher to be competent in every facet of their career.
