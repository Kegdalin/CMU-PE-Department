Department of Physical Education

University Vision

A leading ASEAN university actively committed to the total development of people for a globally sustainable environment and a humane society.

University Mission

To advance the frontier of knowledge through internationalization of education and equitable access to quality instruction, research, extension and production.

College of Education Goals

The College is committed to pursue excellence and total quality education which aims to develop highly competent and morally upright teachers and leaders comparable to international standards in the content, pedagogy and instructional leadership.

College of Education Objectives

  1. To produce highly competent teachers imbued with desirable character traits, equipped with scientific and evidence-based knowledge through a variety of teaching and learning experiences that foster academic breadth and skills training;
  2. To equip teachers to be effective facilitators of learning and responsive to the challenges of the global classroom through keeping abreast with the latest trends in teaching to meet current and future needs of students and community;
  3. To provide teachers with opportunities to the attainment of sustainable development to achieve a clear progression beyond basic and undergraduate education by stressing higher competencies in knowledge application and utilization; and
  4. To train teachers to apply educational theories and practices through research, extension and production endeavors.

Department of Physical Education Objectives

  1. To provide training in Physical Education and related socio-cultural activities of CMU students;
  2. To contribute to Physical Education Theory and Practice through research activities;
  3. To develop awareness of the natural environment and the need for its production and conservation through outdoor and aquatic activities;
  4. To promote Physical Education through an outreach program in sports, physical fitness and socio-cultural activities.

Bachelor of Physical Education Program Objectives

  1. Glocally competent and passionate teachers engaged in the holistic development of learners;
  2. Effective implementers and facilitators of programs and services at all educational levels imbued with desirable values as well as pedagogical content knowledge and skills essential to the development of learners; and,
  3. Actively engaged in research, development, extension and expert services on various educational program.

History of the Department of Physical Education

The Department of Physical Education is among the oldest academic units of Central Mindanao University. Its history is connected to the development of the institution from the Bukidnon National Agricultural School to the present Central Mindanao University.

Over the years, the department has contributed to physical education instruction, sports development, physical fitness, socio-cultural activities, research, and community extension.

The department's academic development includes the offering of Physical Education within the Bachelor of Secondary Education program and the eventual establishment of the standalone Bachelor of Physical Education degree program.

Historical Milestones

1946

The institution's early history began as Bukidnon National Agricultural School.

1952

The institution developed into Mindanao Agricultural College.

1965

Central Mindanao University became the institution's official university identity.

1998

The Bachelor of Secondary Education major in Physical Education was offered.

2017

The standalone Bachelor of Physical Education degree program was proposed.

February 26, 2018

The Board of Regents approved the Bachelor of Physical Education degree program.

Academic Year 2018–2019

Implementation of the standalone Bachelor of Physical Education program began.

2019

Minor revisions were introduced to the academic curriculum.

2022

The first batch of Bachelor of Physical Education students graduated.

2023

Further curriculum revisions were made, including the updated BPEd Prospectus.

Physical Activity Towards Health and Fitness

The department supports Physical Activity Towards Health and Fitness (PATHFIT) courses, which form part of the physical education curriculum for undergraduate students.

PATHFIT 1–4 provides learning experiences that encourage physical activity, movement skills, fitness, and participation in lifelong health-related activities.
